01 / PROFILE

Curiosity is a practical skill.

A systems engineer's perspective on technology, reliability, problem-solving and continuous learning.

My work revolves around operating systems and the infrastructure that supports applications and services. Linux has been a central part of my professional life for roughly two decades, alongside Unix, Windows Server, virtualization, storage and data protection.

I enjoy understanding what happens beneath the interface: how components interact, where failures begin, how data moves, and what makes a system maintainable long after the initial deployment.

My interests extend beyond day-to-day administration into infrastructure architecture, cloud platforms, automation, self-hosted services, open-source software and documenting technical knowledge.

“Discover. Explore. Test. Learn. Repeat.”

This site is a working notebook, not a résumé pretending to be a personality. It collects technical interests, selected experiments and ideas worth revisiting.

Working principles

  • Understand before changing.
  • Prefer evidence over assumptions.
  • Automate repeatable work.
  • Make failure observable.
  • Test recovery, not just backups.
  • Document decisions and trade-offs.
  • Learn continuously; stay curious.
  • Respect the boring, reliable solution.
