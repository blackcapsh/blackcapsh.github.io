06 /

Curiosity is the constant.

PERSONAL OPERATING SYSTEM

I am an infrastructure engineer by trade and a curious person by default. I enjoy taking things apart conceptually, tracing problems to their actual cause, and discovering what happens when you test an idea rather than assume it works.

Technology is not only production clusters and uptime. It is also a Linux installation on a laptop, a self-hosted service, an unfamiliar command, an unfinished script or a question that sends you down an unexpected rabbit hole.

I value substance over noise, fundamentals over hype, and understanding over collecting tools. The work changes; the curiosity stays.
